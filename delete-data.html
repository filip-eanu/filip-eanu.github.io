Delete your data — Feed the Nest

Feed the Nest: Ant Puzzle, by Pop Filip Matei

Feed the Nest has no accounts, and we don’t keep your game progress on any server. Here is how to remove everything connected to the game.

1. Your game progress (on your phone)

Levels, stars, coins, gems, ants and settings are stored only on your device. To delete them, uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Feed the Nest → Storage → Clear storage. This is immediate and can’t be undone.

2. Purchase records (RevenueCat and Google Play)

To ask us to delete the purchase records linked to the game:

  1. Send an email to filip01pop@gmail.com with the subject “Feed the Nest data deletion”.
  2. Include the Google Play order number of a purchase (it starts with “GPA.” and is in your Google Play receipt email), so we can find your records.
  3. We delete your records from RevenueCat within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: the anonymous ID created for the game and its purchase history in RevenueCat. What may be kept: Google keeps its own order and payment records under Google’s terms. Records we must keep by law (for example for tax purposes) are kept only for as long as the law requires, usually up to 10 years, and are not used for anything else. After deletion, purchases can no longer be restored with “Restore purchases”.

3. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google. You can delete or reset your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads), and change your ad choices in the game under Settings → Privacy settings where available. To manage the data Google holds, visit myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
