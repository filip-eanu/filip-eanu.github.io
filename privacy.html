Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Feed the Nest (“the game”) is a mobile puzzle game made by Pop Filip Matei (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is used when you play, why, and the choices you have. If you have any question, write to filip01pop@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device. We don’t receive it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and in US states with privacy laws, you are asked for your choices first, and you can change them at any time.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat helps us confirm and restore purchases.

1. Game data on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, gems, ants and settings are stored locally on your phone. They are not sent to us. They are removed when you uninstall the game (unless your Android device backup restores them on a new phone).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (for example, which ads you saw or tapped).

Where the law requires it, ads are personalized only with your consent. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

You can buy optional items in the game. Payments are processed by Google Play under Google’s terms; we never receive your card or bank details. To confirm purchases and let you restore them, we use RevenueCat, which receives purchase information (the item, a transaction ID, the time and price of the purchase), a random anonymous ID created for the app, and basic device information. RevenueCat’s policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. No other tracking

We do not use other analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell personal information.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK)

We rely on your consent for personalized ads; on our legitimate interest in funding the game with non-personalized ads and preventing fraud; and on the performance of a contract when you make a purchase.

6. Children

Feed the Nest is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help to delete it.

7. Keeping and transferring data

Google and RevenueCat keep data according to their own policies and may process it outside your country, including in the United States, using legal safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Romania: ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro). Because we don’t hold accounts, most requests are best handled through the in-game privacy settings, your Android settings, or by contacting Google and RevenueCat directly. How to delete your data step by step: delete your data. We will gladly help: filip01pop@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
